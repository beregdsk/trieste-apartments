What to ask before you view

  1. È disponibile per una coppia con decorrenza del contratto dal 17 gennaio 2027? Online presence is not availability, and an enquiry does not reserve a flat.
  2. Possiamo prendere entrambi la residenza? Il contratto sarà registrato? A no-residenza restriction is fatal for this household, irrespective of the reason.
  3. È possibile un contratto 3+2 oppure transitorio, non per studenti? Ask duration, lawful temporary reason if applicable, recesso and notice. Student-targeted flat eligibility does not make the student contract compatible.
  4. Quanto costano spese, riscaldamento, acqua, luce, gas, TARI e internet? Request actual annual statements, not an assumed €55 charge. Add deposit and agency commission separately.
  5. Il proprietario collabora per l’idoneità alloggiativa? Trieste’s 2026 form asks for a registered lease, owner conformity declaration and a plan showing room/total areas, heights, uses, bathroom fixtures and extraction details. Published processing deadline: within 45 days. Comune guidance.
